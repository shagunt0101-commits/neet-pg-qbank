# NEET PG + INI-CET PYQ Source Catalog

> Compiled 2026-08-03 from live web research. **NBEMS (NEET PG) and AIIMS (INI-CET) never publish official papers or answer keys.** Every previous-year question in circulation — every PDF, QBank, and app — is a candidate-*recall* reconstruction. Answers are community/AI-verified, not official. Treat them as high-quality practice, not gospel.

## Caveats & recommendations

1. **No official papers exist** — do not chase "official" PDFs; every "answer key" is community-derived.
2. **~30% concept-overlap** between PYQs and live paper; direct verbatim repeats are <8% (2025 style trend: more clinical vignettes + images). So: use PYQs to build *concepts*, not to memorise verbatim.
3. **Free first, paid later.** NEETPGAI + Oncourse + PrepLadder free + Careers360 + AglaSem/DigiNerve + CollegeDunia covers years 2021–2025 for both exams at zero cost.
4. **Telegram repos** are fastest to get full recall papers, but accuracy unvetted — cross-check any answer you plan to memorise.
5. **2026 NEET PG paper** will only exist after **Aug 30, 2026** (recall-based). Rebuild the app bank then.
